# Teacher Answer Key — U.S. History Quarter 2 Review

**TEST VERSION — not approved for students.** The completion percentage indicates filled responses, not correct answers. This key is teacher-facing; do not link it from the student page.

**Learning goal:** explain causes, effects, and perspectives across Westward Expansion, American Imperialism and the origins of WWI.

**Distribution:** 12 Westward Expansion (40%), 12 Imperialism (40%), 6 WWI (20%). 24 multiple-choice questions and 6 short explanations.

## Written-response guidance

These questions have **more than one defensible response**. Accept historically supported explanations rather than expecting a single scripted answer.

### Q11 — we11
Explain one benefit and one harm of westward expansion for two different groups of people.
**Look for:** Accurately identifies two different affected groups, one meaningful benefit and one harm, and explains each with a historical example.

### Q12 — we12
Which had the greatest impact on the West: the Homestead Act, the railroad, or federal policies toward Indigenous peoples? Defend your choice with a specific example.
**Look for:** Selects one of the three choices, explains its importance and supports the judgment with a specific historical consequence. A defensible alternative choice also earns credit.

### Q23 — im11
Explain how an imperialist and an anti-imperialist could interpret the same U.S. action differently. Use one historical example.
**Look for:** Identifies both viewpoints on the SAME example and explains motivations and concerns rather than simply labeling each side.

### Q24 — im12
To what extent did American imperialism reflect or contradict the ideal of self-government? Use evidence from Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines, or Hawaii.
**Look for:** Uses a concrete example from a listed territory and evaluates consent/self-government with historical evidence. A nuanced mixed judgment is appropriate.

### Q29 — ww05
Choose two MAIN causes of WWI. Explain how they reinforced each other and increased tension in Europe.
**Look for:** Names two correct MAIN causes and explains an interaction or reinforcing relationship with accurate historical reasoning.

### Q30 — ww06
Why was the assassination in Sarajevo a trigger rather than the only cause of WWI? Include one long-term cause in your explanation.
**Look for:** Distinguishes the June 1914 assassination as the trigger from an existing underlying condition and shows how the latter made escalation possible.

## Suggested whole-class clarification

1. **Westward Expansion:** Compare who received land, who lost control of land, and how laws reinforced unequal outcomes.
2. **Imperialism:** Ask whether increasing U.S. power necessarily advanced consent of the governed; contrast claims from supporters and critics.
3. **World War I:** Explain that MAIN is a study framework, and leaders’ decisions during the July Crisis were also necessary to understand escalation.
